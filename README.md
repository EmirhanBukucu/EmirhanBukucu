## 👋 About me

My main quest is **indie game development with Godot**. I enjoy turning ideas into playable things, experimenting with mechanics, and building the systems that make a game feel coherent.

The side quests are smaller and a little more opinionated: scripts, utilities, and tiny tools built around a real need. Sometimes the right solution is not another big framework — it's a focused piece of software that does one job the way I want it done.

## 🧭 What guides my work

- **Make it useful.** Build around a real problem, not a checklist of technologies.
- **Keep it focused.** Small systems with clear responsibilities are easier to understand and change.
- **Be opinionated when it helps.** Fewer knobs can mean a clearer tool and a better workflow.
- **Learn by shipping.** Prototypes, experiments, revisions — the work gets better by being made.
